The Position

Bodji helps teams turn messy expert-driven workflows into structured, evidence-aware, AI-ready systems.

Start with one workflow. Prove the value. Then scale.

High-stakes service operations are workflows where requests are complex, evidence matters, expert review is required, and mistakes are expensive. They show up in scientific services, advanced manufacturing, aerospace, energy, insurance, compliance, cybersecurity, healthcare operations, legal services, and enterprise procurement.

Bodji Technologies helps teams convert those workflows into structured, AI-ready systems: workflow catalogs, guided intake, evidence packs, approval paths, and governed AI assistance.

Problem

High-stakes workflows do not fit neatly into generic forms, chatbots, or static PDFs.

Customers need clarity. Internal teams need complete requests. Experts need evidence, versioning, and controlled review paths.

Complex Intake

Requests arrive incomplete

High-value claims, projects, vendor reviews, technical services, or approvals often require multiple clarification cycles before expert review can begin.

Evidence

Documents live everywhere

Specifications, certificates, contracts, reports, control evidence, prior decisions, and supporting documents are difficult to package into one reusable view.

Review

Workflows are hard to replay

Decisions, approvals, exceptions, and customer communications often lack a clean trail suitable for audit visibility, workflow improvement, or AI assistance.

Boundaries

Bodji is not a replacement for your LIMS, QMS, ERP, case-management platform, or validated systems of record.

Bodji begins as a workflow and evidence layer around high-friction expert processes: intake, scoping, review, evidence packaging, and decision support. Production integrations, validation support, regulated data handling, and enterprise deployment are scoped separately.
