The Position

Bodji Technologies helps regulated scientific and diagnostic organizations turn complex services, evidence, and quality workflows into structured, AI-ready digital systems.

Start with one workflow. Prove the value. Then scale.

Scientific service organizations rely on trust: accurate requests, complete evidence, technical review, quality documentation, and repeatable decisions. But many of those workflows still depend on emails, PDFs, spreadsheets, portals, and manual handoffs.

Bodji Technologies helps teams convert those workflows into structured, AI-ready systems: service catalogs, guided intake, evidence packs, approval paths, and governed AI assistance.

Problem

Scientific service complexity does not fit neatly into generic forms, chatbots, or static PDFs.

Customers need clarity. Internal teams need complete requests. Quality and technical teams need evidence, versioning, and control.

Partner Intake

Requests arrive incomplete

Custom diagnostic, biomaterial, QC, or lab-service requests often require multiple clarification cycles before technical review can begin.

Evidence

Documents live everywhere

Certificates, quality credentials, technical notes, references, review notes, and support documents are difficult to package into one reusable view.

Operations

Workflows are hard to replay

Decisions, approvals, exceptions, and customer communications often lack a clean digital trail suitable for workflow improvement or AI assistance.

What Bodji Does

A controlled workflow and evidence layer around existing systems.

Bodji starts at the commercial-technical interface: service discovery, intake, routing, evidence packaging, and review. Production deployment, validation, and regulated records are scoped deliberately when the organization is ready.

Catalog

Service Catalogs

Convert complex scientific services into structured options, constraints, required inputs, outputs, and routing rules.

Intake

Guided Intake Workflows

Collect the right context upfront from partners, customers, or internal teams so technical review starts faster.

Evidence

Evidence Packs

Attach quality documents, certificates, metadata, references, review notes, and trust signals to a request or service line.

AI Layer

Governed AI Assistance

Use AI to structure, summarize, compare, and route work while preserving owner approval, audit visibility, and policy boundaries.
